About CROSSFIRE

CROSSFIRE is not just another monitoring tool. It's a TCAS-grade collision avoidance system purpose-built for the era of parallel AI agents. When Agent A assumes discount is float[0.0, 1.0] and Agent B assumes int[0, 100], CROSSFIRE detects the invariant drift before it reaches production — and issues a Resolution Advisory to correct it.
